## Card-to-card transfers

### What are the fees for card-to-card transfers?

Transfers between the bank's cards are free.

Transfers funded with the customer's own money to cards issued by other
Ukrainian banks are also free.

When a transfer is funded through a credit limit, the fee is 4% of the
transfer amount.

---

## Installment payments

### What is an installment plan?

An installment plan allows the cost of a purchase to be divided into equal
monthly payments.

The fee is 1.9% per month of the purchase amount.

The customer can repay the installment plan early without additional
penalties.

---

## Credit limit

### How can I increase my credit limit?

The credit limit is determined automatically using the bank's internal
customer assessment model.

The decision may be influenced by:

- how regularly the card is used;
- repayment history;
- income level;
- credit history.

A separate application for a credit-limit increase cannot be submitted.

---

## Card top-ups

### How can I top up my card?

A card can be topped up through:

- a bank transfer;
- a card issued by another bank;
- self-service terminals;
- cash deposits through the bank's partners.

---

## Deposits

### Can I open a deposit?

Yes.

A deposit can be opened directly in the mobile application.

The interest rate depends on the deposit term and conditions.

---

## Cashback

### How does cashback work?

Customers can select cashback categories every month.

The cashback rate depends on the selected category and promotional terms.

Accrued cashback is credited automatically after the end of the settlement
period.

---

## Security

### What should I do if my card is lost?

Block the card immediately in the mobile application.

After that, you can issue a replacement card or contact customer support.

---

## Customer support

### How can I contact customer support?

Customer support is available through the chat in the mobile application.

The support service operates 24 hours a day.

---

## Foreign-currency cards

### Can I open a foreign-currency card?

Yes.

The bank supports foreign-currency cards in the currencies available in the
mobile application.

Cards are managed through the mobile application.

---

## Cash withdrawals

### What is the cash-withdrawal fee?

The fee depends on the card type, the source of funds, and the bank's current
pricing terms.

Current conditions are displayed in the mobile application.

---

## PIN change

### How can I change my card PIN?

The PIN can be changed in the card settings within the mobile application.

The new PIN becomes active immediately after confirmation.
